01 · THREE PLANES

Thinking, knowing, and allowing — kept apart.

The separation is the point. Each plane owns its responsibility and nothing else.

CRIPSISAI · THINKS

Decides what should happen

Observes authorized events, keeps active work, assembles context, reasons, recommends, and asks when information is missing.

KYBERNITE · KNOWS

Owns what the enterprise knows

Evidence, provenance, decisions, outcomes, and validated practices — durable, inspectable, customer-owned.

TRILITHIUM · ALLOWS

Decides what is allowed

Identity, authority, policy, approvals, and receipts. Default-deny, deny-precedence, customer isolation, fail-closed.

02 · ACTIVE LEARNING

It learns from the bottom up — and asks.

Repeated behavior is evidence, not truth. Written procedure is evidence, not gospel.

Observe & correlate

Normalizes authorized activity and infers which actions belong to one piece of work.

Hypothesize

Forms a candidate explanation of how work is normally performed — held tentatively.

Question

Initiates clarification on uncertainty, conflict, or an unexplained decision.

Validate

Confirms with an authority, then records a practice with evidence and confidence.

03 · ORGANIZATIONAL MEMORY

More than conversation history.

Memory is structured so a learned practice is inspectable — not opaque vectors.

Episodic memory

What happened in specific cases.

Operational knowledge

Known entities, systems, terms, and relationships.

Practices

How work is normally performed.

Rules & policies

Explicit organizational requirements.

Exceptions

Conditions under which a practice changes.

Evidence & provenance

Why Cripsis believes something — traceable to source.

04 · TRUST & CONTROL

Your intelligence. Your authority. Your walls.

The enterprise owns and controls its intelligence. Cripsis owns the infrastructure that governs it.

Ownership

The model, the data, and the learned intelligence stay yours.

Data never leaves

Segregated in your environment; never used to improve anyone else’s.

Deterministic authority

Default-deny, fail-closed. Every action evaluated before it happens.

Provenance & receipts

Every belief traceable to evidence; every action produces a record.

You set the boundary

You authorize which systems and information Cripsis may access.

Human authority preserved

Learning how work is done does not authorize doing it.

05 · DELEGATION

A deliberately staged autonomy ramp.

Assistance before autonomy. The agent proposes and learns; the control substrate decides.

OBSERVE → ASK

Learn the workflow; question ambiguous or conflicting behavior.

ASSIST → COMPRESS

Retrieve context, summarize, prioritize, draft; automate repetitive preparation.

DELEGATE → OPERATE

Perform bounded approved actions; continue recurring work within authority.

06 · DEPLOYMENT

Install, authorize, observe, expand.

No six-month transformation project first. The mess is part of what Cripsis learns to navigate.

01

Install

02

03

Observe & learn

04

Expand on proof

Broaden coverage and bounded write capability once value is shown.
