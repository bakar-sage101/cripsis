// the doctrine

Intelligence should belong to the enterprise that creates it.

Organizations are told they must surrender their operating knowledge to get the benefits of AI. Cripsis is built on the opposite premise: your intelligence is infrastructure, and infrastructure should be owned.

OWNED

Your intelligence stays yours

Customer-specific knowledge is segregated in your environment — not commoditized into someone else’s model.

GOVERNED

Authority is deterministic

Learning does not confer authority. What Cripsis may do is decided by explicit policy, with a receipt for every action.

COMPOUNDING

It gets more specific over time

Every validated workflow adds institutional intelligence — increasingly specific to how your organization operates.

// principles

How Cripsis is built.

Evidence, not truth

Seeing something five times is evidence — never automatically company policy.

Questions are first-class

Cripsis initiates clarification when it detects uncertainty, conflict, or missing information.

Procedure is evidence

When practice diverges from the written SOP, Cripsis notices — and asks why.

Learning ≠ authority

Knowing how humans perform work does not authorize Cripsis to perform it.

Start with one workflow

Prove the learning mechanism deeply before chasing broad coverage.

Measure operating outcomes

Better prioritization, fewer missed exceptions, less effort, and safe delegation.
