What does “dedicated” mean?

Each plan is its own Cripsis environment with its own resident AI compute. Your intelligence is never shared with or trained into another customer’s system.

How do I choose a tier?

By operators and concurrency. Office and Business suit small and operational teams; Pro and Pro+ add capacity, integrations, and background reasoning for heavier, multi-GPU workloads.

Can it run on our own infrastructure?

Yes — private infrastructure and customer-hosted systems are handled under Enterprise & Gov, alongside regulated-environment and advanced-security requirements.
